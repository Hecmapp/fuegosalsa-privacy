Request account deletion

This policy describes data processing in the Fuego Salsa radio app by HECMAPP, LLC, identified on Android as com.hecmapp.fuegosalsa and on iOS as com.Hema-app.FuegoSalsaApp.

For privacy enquiries or to request deletion of an account and its data, email support@hecmapp.com and identify your request as relating to Fuego Salsa. Do not send your password.

Data and app features

  • Radio and artwork. The app connects to fuegosalsa.radioca.st for audio, songs, statistics and station history. It searches Apple's iTunes service using artist and song names and downloads artwork. These connections send your IP address and technical request data to the respective services.
  • Analytics. Update 4.6.0 (23) permanently disables Google Analytics for Firebase collection through native Android and iOS configuration. Earlier versions may use Analytics to record opening, sessions, playback, displayed songs, sharing and notification interactions, together with identifiers and technical data according to their configuration. Disabling Analytics does not disable AdMob, notifications, remote configuration or the promotion counters described here.
  • Advertising. Google AdMob processes IP, interactions, diagnostics and available identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google UMP manages advertising privacy choices. Processing depends on platform, configuration and applicable preferences.
  • Notifications and configuration. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses installation and registration identifiers for station notifications; Apple's APNs participates on iOS. Firebase Remote Config receives identifiers and country, language, time zone and version information to distribute metadata display rules.
  • Content and promotions. Firestore delivers DJ names and schedules, broadcast status and promotions. The app sends promotion view and click increments. The counters do not contain a listener identifier added by the code; this does not remove Firebase's technical data processing.
  • Fonts and caches. The app may download fonts from Google servers, which receive connection data. The device stores up to five recent station songs, including artist, title and artwork, alongside image and font caches and local SDK session and preference data. The song list can update without pressing Play.

DJ and administrator accounts

Listening does not require registration. DJ and administrator accounts are internal accounts for authorized Fuego Salsa employees, provisioned by the owner; access is not open to the public. They authenticate with email and password through Firebase Authentication. The account directory stores identifier, email, last sign-in, permissions, blocking status and creation date. Update 4.6.0 (23) does not add the creator's email when provisioning new accounts. Firebase Authentication also processes IP and technical authentication data to operate the service and prevent abuse.

Administrators can enter DJ names and schedules, promotional text and links, and select images from their photo library for publication as banners. Images upload to Firebase Storage. The code in update 4.6.0 (23) does not add the administrator's email to image metadata. Banners and schedules published by the station may be displayed to listeners; this does not make administrative access or the employee account directory a public feature.

This data authenticates station staff and supports station content administration. Signing out or blocking an account does not delete it.

Recipients and external links

Recipients include Google/Firebase/AdMob, Apple for artwork and iOS notifications, the station's streaming service, and services delivering images and fonts. Sharing passes the song name and store link to the destination you select. Opening the website, a promotion or a sponsor link communicates data to that destination, subject to its policy.

Information sent to Google for advertising and measurement is processed according to the services used, their configuration and applicable preferences. Advertisers and destination websites may also receive information when you interact with their ads or links. See how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

Your choices

You can manage notification permission and advertising identifiers in device settings. If UMP requires an advertising privacy option, the app shows Privacidad de anuncios (advertising privacy) to reopen it. These advertising choices do not themselves disable other SDKs or services, such as notifications and remote configuration. Analytics deactivation in update 4.6.0 (23) is independent of those choices.

You can remove local data and caches using system controls, subject to their operation and backups. Clearing local data or uninstalling does not itself delete data already processed by remote services.

To enquire, correct data or request deletion of data or a DJ/admin account, email support@hecmapp.com. Describe your request and, for an account, provide its email address. Do not publish personal data in public GitHub issues.

Delete your Fuego Salsa account

You can request deletion without installing or opening the app. Email support@hecmapp.com with the subject “Fuego Salsa: account deletion request”. Include the email associated with your account and identify any personal data or content you want deleted. Never send your password.

The operator checks the requester's identity before removing the account and associated data. A reply from the associated email may be requested; your password is never requested. This channel also supports earlier app versions, status enquiries and incomplete attempts. Providers' technical data is subject to the retention conditions explained below.

In update 4.6.0 (23), you can also open staff access, choose Mi cuenta (My account), then Eliminar mi cuenta (Delete my account). This option remains available for a blocked session or one whose profile was already removed. Listening does not require an account.

Retention and deletion

Accounts are retained to authenticate staff for as long as needed. In update 4.6.0 (23), Mi cuenta → Eliminar mi cuenta (My account → Delete my account) removes the directory profile and deletes the Firebase Authentication identity after password confirmation. If the final step fails, the app explains the partial result and allows a retry. Blocking or signing out does not delete the account.

Schedules, promotions and images published by the station are shared content and are not automatically deleted when an access account is deleted. If this content includes your personal data, identify it in a request to support@hecmapp.com for review and deletion where applicable. The current flow does not add the account identifier to that content.

Device history and caches remain subject to platform storage and controls. Retention of technical and measurement data processed by Google, Apple, the radio service and other network destinations depends on the service, its configuration and its policies. This policy sets no uniform period for that data or guaranteed deadline for handling manual deletion. Clearing or uninstalling the app does not itself remove remote copies.

Transmission and security

The radio and artwork search destinations included in the app use HTTPS. Firebase and advertising SDKs document encryption in transit. External images and links can depend on content configured by the station. These features do not guarantee absolute security.
